DMCA Takedown Notice

Last updated: January 2026

Audiory respects intellectual property rights. If you believe content on Audiory infringes your copyright, you may request removal by sending a DMCA notice to our designated contact below.

1. Where to Send Notices

Email: legal@audiory.site

2. What to Include in Your DMCA Notice

To be valid, your notice should include:

  • Your full legal name and contact information (email and/or phone)
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim was infringed
  • The exact Audiory URL(s) or content details where the alleged infringement appears
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate, under penalty of perjury
  • Your physical or electronic signature (typing your legal name is acceptable for electronic signature)

3. What Happens Next

  • We review the report and may remove or disable access to the content.
  • We may notify the uploader/producer of the claim.
  • Repeat infringers may have their accounts suspended or terminated.

4. Counter-Notification

If you believe your content was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may send a counter-notification to legal@audiory.site. Your counter-notice should include:

  • Your name and contact details
  • Identification of the removed content and where it appeared
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the content was removed due to mistake or misidentification
  • Consent to jurisdiction where required by applicable law
  • Your physical or electronic signature

5. Important Note

Submitting false claims may result in legal consequences. If you are unsure whether content is infringing, consider contacting a legal professional before submitting a DMCA notice.
