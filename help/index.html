How buyers download after payment Where to find your files, what to do if download fails.
BUYERS

After payment, your order is saved and your download unlock is created.

  • Go to Dashboard → Orders
  • Open your latest order
  • Click Download (Beat file) and License (PDF)

If download fails:

  • Refresh and try again
  • Use a different browser
  • Send us your order ID using the chat button
Payments: PayPal & M-Pesa (what happens) What “paid” means and why a payment may not unlock instantly.
PAYMENTS

PayPal: you approve → PayPal captures → Audiory creates unlocks.

M-Pesa: you confirm STK → callback updates order status → unlock created.

If you paid but didn’t unlock, message support with:

  • Your account email
  • Order ID (or receipt)
  • Screenshot of the payment
How producers upload beats / kits Upload, set license prices, publish.
PRODUCERS
  • Go to Producer Dashboard → Upload
  • Add title, BPM, genre, mood
  • Upload audio file + artwork (optional)
  • Set license prices (Basic / Pro / Exclusive)
  • Click Publish

Tip: Keep your filenames clean and short (no weird symbols).

Wallet, fees, and payouts How your balance grows and when you can withdraw.
PRODUCERS

When a beat/kit is paid, Audiory credits your wallet (after platform fee).

  • Balance shows in Producer Dashboard → Wallet
  • Withdraw when you reach your minimum amount

If a payout is stuck, send your payout ID using chat.

Account & login issues Reset password, verify email, fix session problems.
ACCOUNT
  • Try Forgot password on the login page
  • Check spam folder for verification emails
  • Logout and login again

If you’re blocked, send your email + screenshot in chat.
