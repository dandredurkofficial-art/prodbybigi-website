Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common Audiory questions.

I paid but the beat did not unlock

+
Sometimes payment providers take a few minutes to verify. If your beat is still locked, contact support below and include your screenshot or order ID.

How do I upload beats?

+
Login to your producer dashboard, click upload beat, add your files, pricing, artwork and licenses then publish.

How long do withdrawals take?

+
Most M-Pesa withdrawals process within minutes while PayPal payouts may take longer depending on provider delays.

Can I edit uploaded beats?

+
Yes. Producers can edit titles, prices, tags, cover art, descriptions and licenses anytime from dashboard.

My download link expired

+
Contact Audiory support with your email and order details. We can resend your download access.
