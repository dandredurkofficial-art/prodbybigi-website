Cookies Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how Audiory (“we”, “our”, or “Platform”) uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to recognize you when you visit our marketplace. It details what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control our use of them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text data files stored directly inside your internet browser directory when you load specific websites. They act as a memory log for platforms, allowing us to keep you logged in safely, prevent CSRF attacks, maintain items inside your shopping cart, and keep customized audio options running across multiple system navigation reloads.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

We separate our tracking tools into three core groups based on function and platform dependency:

  • Essential Cookies: These are critical system operations data links required strictly to run basic functionalities. Without these, core services like account logins, processing secure transactions, and routing user streaming bandwidth cannot execute reliably.
  • Analytics Cookies: These tools track aggregate user activity patterns, traffic entry nodes, page load speeds, and audio player error logs. This data remains completely non-identifiable and serves purely to assist us in building structural code upgrades.
  • Preference Cookies: These tools allow our interface code to store local custom states you select, such as preserving dashboard toggle parameters, remembering continuous volume slider settings, and keeping preferred sorting parameters inside beat directories.

3. Session vs. Persistent Cookies

Cookies can also be classified based on how long they remain active on your machine:

  • Session Cookies: These are temporary strings deleted instantly from your temporary system cache the moment your web browser process is fully shut down.
  • Persistent Cookies: These remain inside your browser storage path for a set duration or until wiped out manually by your cleaning processes. They allow us to remember your setup preferences next time you open the ecosystem.

4. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to decline or remove cookies via their integrated configuration consoles. Be aware that disabling cookies completely may render parts of Audiory's audio player, checkout stack, and user panels unusable.

To make manual adjustments, navigate directly into your specific desktop or mobile client settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Third-Party Cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Apple Safari: Settings → Privacy → Block All Cookies
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions → Manage and Delete Cookies

We may occasionally refresh this policy to reflect shifts in technology protocols, compliance regulations, or optimization frameworks. We recommend checking back periodically to stay updated on how we handle privacy.
