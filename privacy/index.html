Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Welcome to Audiory (“Audiory”, “we”, “us”, “our”). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your information when you use our website, services, and features (collectively, the “Services”).

By using Audiory, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

A) Information you provide

  • Account details: name/username, email address, password (stored securely), profile details.
  • Producer content: beats, sound kits, artwork, titles, genres, descriptions, and related metadata.
  • Customer support: messages, requests, and information you share when contacting us.
  • Marketing/communications: preferences you choose (e.g., email updates).

B) Information collected automatically

  • Usage data: pages viewed, clicks, searches, time spent, and other activity on the Services.
  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system, timestamps.
  • Cookies & similar technologies: used for functionality, analytics, and preference storage (see Section 6).

C) Payment and transaction information

  • Purchases: order details, items purchased, transaction status, and payment confirmations.
  • Important: We do not store your full card details. Payments are processed by third-party payment providers (e.g., PayPal), and their privacy policies apply to payment processing.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the Services (accounts, profiles, uploads, marketplace browsing).
  • Process purchases, licensing, and downloads (including confirmations and receipts).
  • Secure the platform, prevent fraud, enforce our Terms, and protect users.
  • Improve performance, features, and user experience (analytics and debugging).
  • Communicate with you (service updates, support responses, important notices).
  • Comply with legal obligations (tax, accounting, lawful requests, dispute resolution).

3. Legal Bases (Where Required)

Where laws require a legal basis, we rely on: contract necessity (to provide Services), legitimate interests (security, improvement), consent (optional marketing/cookies where required), and legal obligations (compliance).

4. How We Share Your Information

We may share information only as needed in these cases:

  • Service providers: hosting, analytics, storage, and technical providers who help run Audiory.
  • Payment processors: to complete transactions (e.g., PayPal).
  • Legal and safety reasons: if required by law or to protect our users and Services.
  • Business transfers: if Audiory is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale (with notice where required).

We do not sell your personal data in the traditional sense. If laws define “sale” differently, we will comply with applicable requirements.

5. Producer Content & Public Information

Producer profiles and uploaded content (such as beats, artwork, titles, and producer names) may be publicly visible. Please do not upload personal information you don’t want made public.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working, remember preferences, and understand usage. Some cookies are essential; others may be optional depending on your location and settings.

  • Essential cookies: required for login, security, and core functionality.
  • Preference cookies: remember settings (e.g., cookie choices).
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand usage and improve the Services.

You can control cookies via your browser settings and, where available, our cookie settings page: Cookie Settings.

7. Data Processing, Licensing & Content Rights

Audiory is a marketplace that allows producers to upload content (such as beats and sound kits) and allows artists/customers to discover and purchase licenses or downloads. The way we process data and content includes:

  • Uploads and hosting: When a producer uploads beats, artwork, or sound kits, we store and display that content to provide the marketplace experience, show previews, and enable purchases/downloads where available.
  • Licensing and order fulfillment: When you purchase a license or download, we process order details (item, price, timestamp, and confirmation status) to fulfill the transaction and provide access to the purchased content where applicable.
  • Preview/playback data: We may record basic interaction events (like plays, clicks, or downloads) to improve the platform, prevent abuse, and help producers understand what’s trending.
  • Content ownership: Producers remain responsible for ensuring they have rights to upload and sell content. Audiory does not claim ownership of user-uploaded content; we only process and display it to operate the Services.
  • Enforcement and fraud prevention: We may use information (including usage and transaction signals) to detect suspicious behavior, prevent fraud, enforce terms, and protect the community.

For more details about usage permissions and licenses, please refer to our Terms.

8. Data Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Retention periods vary depending on the type of data and purpose.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information. However, no system is 100% secure. Please use a strong password and keep it confidential.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than where you live (for example, where our hosting or service providers operate). We take steps to protect your data in line with this Privacy Policy and applicable laws.

11. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct or update inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal/contract requirements).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent for optional processing where applicable.

To request access, deletion, or corrections, contact us using the emails below.
